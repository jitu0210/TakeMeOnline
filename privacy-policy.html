Privacy policy

TakeMeOnline collects only the details needed to reply to you and deliver your project. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not sell your data.

Last updated 1 November 2026

Who does this policy cover?

This policy explains how TakeMeOnline ("we", "us") handles personal data when you visit takemeonline.com, send us an enquiry or become a client. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law.

It does not cover the websites we build for clients. Each client decides what data their own website collects and is responsible for its own privacy notice.

What data do we collect?

  • Enquiry details: your name, business name, phone number, chosen package and anything you write in your message.
  • Messages: your phone number or email address and what you send us on WhatsApp, by email or on a call.
  • Project details: business information, addresses, photos, logos, account access and contact details you give us so we can build your website and set up your profiles.
  • Billing records: invoices, payment confirmations and any tax details needed for them.
  • Server logs: our website host may automatically record technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to keep the website secure and running.

How does the contact form work?

The form on our website does not save your details on a server. When you choose to send it, it opens WhatsApp or your email app with your enquiry filled in. We only receive your enquiry if you then send that message.

Does this website use cookies or trackers?

No. This website sets no cookies and has no analytics, advertising pixels or third-party trackers. Our fonts and images are served from our own website. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

Why do we use your data?

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a plan and price.
  • To deliver your project, including your website, domain, hosting, Google Business Profile and social media profiles.
  • To send invoices, keep records and meet our legal and tax duties.
  • To give you support and tell you about things that affect your project, such as domain and hosting renewals.
  • To keep our website and services secure.

We use your data because you gave it to us for these purposes or because the law requires it. We do not use it for anything unrelated without asking you first.

Who do we share your data with?

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only when needed, with:

  • Service providers: domain registrars, hosting companies, and our email and messaging providers, including WhatsApp.
  • Platforms you ask us to set up: such as Google Business Profile, Instagram and Facebook. Details you choose to publish there become public.
  • Professional advisers: such as our accountants, when needed for our records.
  • Authorities: when the law requires us to.

Some of these providers may store or process data outside India. Each provider also handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long do we keep your data?

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to talk to you about your project. We keep project details while we are working with you, and billing records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. After that, we delete the data or remove anything that identifies you.

How do we protect your data?

Our website is served over a secure connection, and we limit access to your data to the people working on your project. No online service is completely secure, so we also ask you not to send passwords or other sensitive details by ordinary email. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

What are your rights?

Under Indian law, you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update your data.
  • Ask us to delete your data, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw your consent. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it, but we may be unable to continue your project without the data.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to act.
  • Make a complaint to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our answer.

What about children's data?

Our services are for businesses and are not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has sent us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

How can you contact us about your data?

To use your rights or raise a complaint, email our grievance contact at hello@takemeonline.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Will this policy change?

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. We will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top. Our terms of service explain how our projects work.
