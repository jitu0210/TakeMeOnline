What are the pros and cons of taking a business online?

Going online helps more customers find, trust and contact your business. The trade-offs are an upfront cost, some upkeep and results that build over time.

Pros of going online

  • More customers find you You can appear when nearby people search on Google and Maps.
  • Room to grow sales You can reach people who never walk past your shop.
  • Open at any hour Customers can enquire or order after you close.
  • More trust Photos, prices and reviews help people choose you.

Cons to plan for

  • Upfront cost You pay for setup first. Our fixed price means no surprises.
  • Some upkeep Hours, prices and photos need to stay current. We show you how.
  • Results take time Search visibility usually builds over weeks or months, not overnight.
  • Public reviews Unhappy customers can post too, so you'll need to reply to reviews.

Questions about going online

What does TakeMeOnline do?

TakeMeOnline helps offline businesses build a digital presence. We create your website, set up your Google Business Profile, improve your local SEO and set up your social media, so customers can find and contact you online.

How much does it cost to take a business online?

Our one-time packages start at ₹9,999 for a one-page website with a Google Business Profile. The Growth package costs ₹24,999 and the Pro package costs ₹49,999. The price is fixed and agreed before work begins.

What are the pros and cons of taking a business online?

The main pros are that more customers can find you on Google and Maps, you can receive enquiries at any hour, and photos, prices and reviews build trust. The main cons are the upfront cost, the upkeep needed to keep information current, search results that take time to build, and public reviews that need replies.

Will going online increase my sales?

It can, because more people can find and contact your business, but it is not guaranteed. Results depend on your location, your competition, your offer and how well you keep your online profiles updated.

Why not buy a ₹2,000 to ₹3,000 website?

Websites at ₹2,000 to ₹3,000 are often copied templates with no SEO, slow loading and no support after delivery, so they rarely bring in customers. TakeMeOnline is a real development team that builds your website for speed and local search, and supports you through launch.

How long does it take to get my business online?

Starter and Growth projects go live within 7 days of receiving your business details and photos. Pro projects take up to 14 days because they include more pages and features.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. You tell us about your business and share your photos. We handle the design, setup and publishing, then show you how to make simple updates.

Will my business show up on Google?

We set up your website and Google Business Profile so your business can appear in Google Search and Maps. Google controls its own rankings, so no honest provider can guarantee the top position, but these steps give you a strong foundation for local search.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain name, website content and business profiles are registered in your business's name, so they stay yours even if you stop working with us.
